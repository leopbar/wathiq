# LEN-POL-002 — Income evidence: salary certificates

> SYNTHETIC POLICY. Written for this demonstration. Not a real bank policy.
> This is the policy for use case 2, which was added by configuration alone.

## §1.1 Salary components

A salary certificate states a basic salary and a total salary. The total is the basic plus
allowances, so it can never be lower than the basic. Where it is, one of the two figures has
been read from the wrong line and neither may be used for an affordability calculation.

## §1.2 Employer

The certificate must name the employer. Without it the certificate cannot be verified against
anything, and it is returned to the customer.

## §1.4 Age of the certificate

Income evidence must be current. A certificate issued more than ninety days ago is not accepted
for a new lending decision; the customer is asked for a recent one.

## §1.5 Future dating

A certificate dated in the future is either a misread date or a forged document. It is always
referred to an officer, never processed automatically.

## §2.2 Salary transfer account

Where an IBAN is given it must be a valid UAE IBAN: the country code AE followed by
twenty-one digits. The written form may group the digits with spaces. An IBAN that does not fit
the format is recorded as a warning and confirmed with the customer before any salary transfer
instruction is set up.

## §3.1 Consistency with the employment letter

Where an employment letter is also on file, the designation and the employer must agree with
the salary certificate. A disagreement is referred to an officer.
