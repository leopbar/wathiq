# KYC-POL-006 — Cross-document consistency and beneficial ownership

> SYNTHETIC POLICY. Written for this demonstration. Not a real bank policy.

## §1.1 Memorandum of association

The memorandum states the company name, the share capital and the shareholders. The company
name is the value the trade licence is checked against, so a memorandum without a readable
company name cannot be used.

## §2.1 Company name across documents

The company name on the trade licence and the name on the memorandum must refer to the same
entity. Differences of legal-form abbreviation — LLC, L.L.C., FZ-LLC — are spelling, not
substance, and are not discrepancies. A difference in the substantive name is a discrepancy and
is resolved against the company registry before the file is refreshed.

## §2.2 Personal names across documents

A person's name on the Emirates ID and on the passport must refer to the same person.
Transliteration differences are expected, particularly in Arabic names, and are not by
themselves a discrepancy. Where the names cannot be reconciled, the file is not refreshed until
an officer has established which document is correct.

## §3.1 Sanctions screening

Every named individual and every named entity on the file is screened before the refresh
completes. A possible match is never resolved automatically. It is referred to an officer, and
where the officer cannot rule it out, to compliance. The absence of a match is recorded as
positively as the presence of one, with the list and the date.

## §4.1 Beneficial ownership

Every shareholder named in the memorandum must have an identity document on the file. A
shareholder with no passport and no Emirates ID makes the refresh incomplete by definition,
regardless of how well the other documents were read. The case is routed to an officer, who
requests the missing document.

## §4.2 Shareholding percentages

Shareholding percentages must total one hundred. A total that does not is either a missing
shareholder or a misread number, and an officer decides which before the file is refreshed.
